About This Builder +

This website creates a personalized prompt from your answers. It doesn't search for jobs itself, and it doesn't need an account.

You copy the prompt into Claude. Claude uses your Indeed connection to read your Indeed résumé, research current job postings, and build your command center as a dashboard you can come back to.

Your answers stay in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server or shared with other visitors.
